Overland: a warmer, more confident Salt & Sun Tours

Not a new brand — the one this site already has, sharpened. One warm terracotta-to-ochre hue family, an editorial serif with real character, and a "hand-assembled" motion signature carried through every section instead of a few.

01

Logo & Wordmark

The existing mark (the arc-over-circle "sun above the dunes" icon already in Header.astro) stays — it's a good, simple, on-brand symbol. What changes is the wordmark's typeface, moving from generic Georgia to Libre Bodoni, and the mark finally getting a defined reversed/tint treatment for dark and tinted backgrounds instead of only ever appearing one way.

Salt & Sun Tours Primary — on sand
Salt & Sun Tours Reversed — on earth-900
Salt & Sun Tours On tinted sand-100
Clear space
Minimum clear space around the mark equals the mark's own height (32px at reference size) on every side — never let nav links or copy crowd it.
Minimum size
Mark never renders below 22px tall; below that the arc reads as a smudge. Wordmark never sets smaller than --fs-md (1.15rem).
Color
Mark is always a single flat currentColor stroke — terracotta-600 on light, sand-50 on dark. Never fill it, never gradient it.

03

Typography

Georgia was a placeholder that happened to work — Libre Bodoni is the deliberate choice: same warm editorial register, but with real bracketed-serif character that reads as considered rather than "whatever the system had." Public Sans replaces the plain system-font stack for the same reason: warmth without losing speed.

Display — Headlines, section titles, tour names

font-family: 'Libre Bodoni', Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif;

Two home bases, one coast

Rule — 400 weight only for headings at any size; reserve 600 italic for the one "special occasion" moment per page (currently the closing CTA). Never set body copy in this font.

Body — Paragraphs, labels, buttons, UI

font-family: 'Public Sans', -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif;

We run every tour out of Swakopmund and Walvis Bay.

ABCDEFGHIJKLM abcdefghijklm 0123456789 &—"…"

Rule — 400 for paragraphs, 600 for eyebrows/labels/buttons (always uppercase + 0.05–0.12em tracking at that weight). Never 700 in body copy — it reads as shouting against the serif's warmth.

Voice & Copy

Headline
Two home bases, one stretch of coast and desert.
Fragment sentences over complete ones. Periods, not exclamation marks — confidence, not enthusiasm.
Eyebrow
Tour Programs
2–3 words, never a full sentence.
Body copy
We run every tour out of Swakopmund and Walvis Bay — no call center, no middleman.
Second person or first-person-plural "we." Specific nouns (place names, numbers) over vague ones ("amazing experiences").
CTA button
Start Booking
Imperative verb first. Never "Learn More" or "Continue" — say what happens next.
Stat label
31 tours, adventures & combos
Number leads. No article ("a", "the") in the label.
FAQ / trust answer
"No call center. Reach out and you'll hear back from someone who actually knows the dunes."
Answer the anxiety directly before the feature — never lead with the company's own claim about itself.

12

Do's & Don'ts

✓ DO
Use the 4 / 10 / 18px radius scale — 10px for cards and buttons, 18px for full-bleed photo cards.
✗ DON'T
Introduce a one-off radius like 2px — this already happened once (About/Gallery/Program photos) and reads as a different, colder decision.
✓ DO
Depth via warm-tinted shadow — rgba(58,35,28, …), never pure black.
✗ DON'T
Use a neutral/black shadow — it instantly reads as generic UI chrome instead of this brand.
✓ DO
One warm gradient family for emphasis fills (buttons, glow).
✗ DON'T
Drop in a dark, unrelated background for a "new" section — this is exactly the dark-brown mistake already caught and reverted once this session.
✓ DO
Section headline
Libre Bodoni for headings only.
✗ DON'T
A whole paragraph of body copy set in the display serif reads slower and feels precious rather than warm.
Never set a full paragraph in Libre Bodoni.
✓ DO
Start Booking
One terracotta CTA per view — the single loudest thing on the screen.
✗ DON'T
Book NowSee ToursContact
Three competing primary buttons in one view — nothing reads as "the" action.
✓ DO
sand-50 everywhere
Keep the sand-to-white gradient consistent section to section.
✗ DON'T
Alternate background themes section-by-section — it fragments the page into unrelated blocks.
✓ DO
"No call center, no middleman."
Direct, specific, confident.
✗ DON'T
"We strive to deliver world-class experiences tailored to your needs."
Hedging, corporate, could be any company on earth.

13

Motion

The signature motion idea is assembling — things settle into a hand-placed position rather than simply fading in place, echoing the tours page's map that pieces itself together and the (currently orphaned) constellation lines between photo cutouts. Every section should carry a taste of that, not just the generic fade the newest section currently uses alone.

Signature entrance
Alternating photo/copy rows: photo settles from scale(1.04), copy trails ~150ms behind on a small y rise — reuses the existing setupTourRows technique instead of a generic fade.
Connective motif
A thin hand-drawn line (stroke-dashoffset) connecting sequential items — revives the constellation idea as the throughline for lists like Tour Programs, instead of letting it stay retired.
Duration & easing
600–900ms, power2.out, for one-shot scroll entrances (these are narrative, not click feedback — longer durations are correct here). Hover/click feedback stays 150–300ms.
Reduced motion
Every effect gets a matching prefers-reduced-motion branch that renders the final state directly — no exceptions, matching the discipline already present sitewide.
Forbidden
No motion invented purely for a new section that isn't reused or extended from an existing technique — the point is one coherent motion vocabulary, not a new idea per section.
