Salt & Sun Tours — the site's motion is more careful than its newest section

A warm, photo-led Astro marketing site for a two-basecamp Namibian tour operator. The pre-existing page (tours.astro, the old homepage sections) carries unusually hand-tuned, reduced-motion-safe motion; the homepage's just-rebuilt About/Gallery/Tour Programs block hasn't caught up to that bar yet, and a chunk of the old motion code is now running for nothing.

Astro 7 static site · GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis · src/scripts/motion.ts (920 lines)

2 Critical 2 Important 3 Opportunities

Overall

This is not an AI-slop codebase — it's the opposite problem. Someone hand-built genuinely distinctive motion (a map that assembles itself piece by piece, constellation lines drawing between photo cutouts, direction-aware panel entrances) with careful reduced-motion parity on nearly every effect. But a recent homepage rewrite deleted the sections that motion lived in without touching the script that drives it, so roughly a quarter of motion.ts now runs against elements that don't exist, and the new homepage content that replaced them ships with none of that craft — just the generic scroll-fade every other section already uses.

Orphaned motion — a quarter of motion.ts now animates nothing

What Eight setup functions (setupAboutCarousel, setupTestimonialCarousel, setupAdventureTilt, setupAdventureRise, setupToursTeaserSlide, setupWhereWeGoReveal, setupPhilosophyCluster, setupStatsCountUp) query selectors that no longer exist anywhere in the site after the homepage was rebuilt — they all safely no-op, but they still run, register listeners, and ship as dead weight. One whole component, AboutCarousel.astro, is now unreferenced.

Why it matters This is the single biggest lever in the whole audit: the motion that got orphaned was the site's most distinctive work — a hand-drawn constellation between photo cutouts, a map that assembles itself, a pointer-tilt on cards. It didn't get simplified away on purpose; it just lost its DOM.

Recommended motion Don't just delete the dead functions — port their best ideas onto the new sections before removing them (see the "reclaimed budget" demo). Then delete what doesn't get reused, and remove AboutCarousel.astro.

Uniform fade-up is now the only entrance on the page

What With the bespoke sections gone, setupReveals (opacity + y:40 + power2.out, 800ms) is the entrance for literally every remaining section — About, Gallery, Tour Programs, and Section.astro's default. Four-plus distinct components sharing one identical entrance is exactly the uniformity the checklist flags.

Why it matters The alternating-row pattern in About and Tour Programs is visually the site's signature layout (used nowhere as a generic list) — but it animates exactly like a generic list, so the layout's own structure (photo vs. copy, left vs. right) isn't reflected in how it enters.

Recommended motion Reuse setupTourRows's technique (already built for this exact photo+copy alternating shape) instead of the generic reveal: photo settles from a slight scale, copy trails in ~150ms behind it.

Issues to address
CriticalEmil

Hero autoplay has no keyboard/touch pause path

What setupHeroPanelSlideshow cycles the hero's 7 photo panels every 2.8s and only pauses on mouseenter/mouseleave — no focusin/focusout, no visible pause control. A keyboard-only or touch-only visitor cannot stop it.

Why it matters This is the exact accessibility gap the site's own later component already solved: setupTourRecapSlideshow, built for the same "auto-cycling slides" job, explicitly adds focusin/focusout alongside hover, with a code comment stating why ("not hover alone, which means nothing on touch"). The fix already exists in this codebase — it just never got backported to the first slideshow.

Recommended motion Add the same focusin/focusout pair to setupHeroPanelSlideshow, and surface a small always-visible pause toggle (the recap slideshow's dots already give a click-to-jump path — the hero panels currently have no equivalent control at all).

New section photos have zero hover response

What Every photo in About, Gallery, and Tour Programs is fully static on hover — no scale, no scrim, nothing. Every other photo-forward pattern on the site (AdventureCard, the tours-page map pieces) has some hover/interaction response.

Why it matters On a fine-pointer device this reads as "did that just not load," not "static by design" — especially next to the Gallery grid, which is the one section on the whole site most clearly meant to invite browsing.

Recommended motion A restrained scale(1.06) + soft scrim on hover, matching the existing AdventureCard language (150-200ms minimum per Jakub's rule) — not the full tilt effect, just parity with the rest of the site's photo hover vocabulary.
